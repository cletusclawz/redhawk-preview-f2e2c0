4Facts to confirm before launch

  • Hours. Your site, Yelp and Apple Maps show different hours. What are the official grille, bar, Sunday and holiday hours?
  • Open jobs. Which roles are really hiring, who gets the applications (email), and is there a number for "Text JOBS to apply"?
  • Private parties. Capacity and minimums for each space, and which email gets party requests?
  • Reservations & forms. Where should table requests and contact forms be delivered?
  • Menu fixes. OK to correct 9 small typos? ("Brussel" → "Brussels" ×3, "Com Bacon" → "Corn, Bacon", "Tertilla" → "Tortilla", "Served with Fits" → "Chips", "House Pasto" → "Pesto", "Bow" → "Bowl", one comma). Also send the happy hour and cocktail menus.
  • Wording. OK to say "the biggest patio in the area" and "20+ years"? What year did Redhawk open?
  • Photos. OK to use photos from your own Facebook, Instagram and Google listing on the site?
  • Access. Domain, hosting and WordPress admin, needed to launch. Also Google Business Profile manager access, so we can fix your listing's photos and hours.
