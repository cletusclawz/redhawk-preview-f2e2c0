Questions

Good to know.

How many guests can you host?

It depends on the space and the season. Tell us your headcount and we'll recommend the right setup.

Do you have packages or minimums?

Menus and pricing are built around your event. Send a request and we'll follow up with options.

Can we use the patio?

Yes, in season. We'll plan an indoor backup with you in case Ohio weather has other ideas.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekends fill up first, especially in summer and the holidays. The earlier the better.

Can I just call?

Absolutely. Call 440-354-4040 and ask about private events.
