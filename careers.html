FAQ

Before you apply.

Do I need a resume?

No. A resume is welcome but not required. Tell us about yourself in the form.

Do I need experience?

Not for every role. Dish, host and bar-back are great places to start. We'll train the right people.

What availability do you need?

Weekends and evenings are our busiest. Check what works for you in the form and we'll match shifts.

How fast will I hear back?

A manager reviews every application. We aim to reach out quickly when your availability fits our needs.

Can I apply in person?

Yes. Stop in and ask for a manager, or apply here first so we have your info.
